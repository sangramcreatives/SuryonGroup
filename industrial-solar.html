Industrial FAQs

☀️ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT INDUSTRIAL SOLAR

How much solar capacity does an industrial facility need? ▼
There is no standard capacity. System size is engineered around electricity consumption, daytime load profile, transformer capacity, available rooftop/land area, and DISCOM net metering regulations.
Can solar power my factory during the night? ▼
Solar panels do not generate power at night. Night power requirements are supplied by your grid connection (offset by daytime export credits) or dedicated battery storage systems.
How is industrial solar ROI calculated? ▼
ROI is calculated using system EPC investment, HT electricity tariff, annual kWh PVsyst generation, 40% depreciation tax savings, and Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) over 25 years.
