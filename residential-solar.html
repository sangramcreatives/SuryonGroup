Home Solar FAQs

☀️ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT RESIDENTIAL SOLAR

How much does residential solar installation cost? ▼
Cost depends on required kW capacity, panel type (DCR Mono PERC), inverter rating, and mounting height. Connect with us on WhatsApp for a customized estimate.
How many solar panels does my home need? ▼
It depends on your electricity bill and panel wattage (e.g. 540W / 550W panels). A 3 kW home system uses 5 to 6 panels.
Is government subsidy available? ▼
Eligible residential homeowners receive up to ₹78,000 direct subsidy under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.
