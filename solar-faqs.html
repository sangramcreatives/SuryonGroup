☀️ Solar Questions, Answered Clearly

Thinking about solar but still have questions?

You're not alone. Solar is a long-term investment, and it's worth understanding costs, savings, installation process, subsidy, maintenance and performance before making a decision.

How Much Does a Solar System Cost? ▼
There is no single price for every installation. Total cost depends on system capacity (kW), panel type (Mono PERC DCR), inverter rating, mounting structure height, electrical wiring, and installation conditions. Connect with us on WhatsApp for an accurate project estimate based on your monthly bill.
How Much Can I Save With Solar? ▼
Your potential savings depend on monthly electricity consumption, tariff, solar capacity, generation, roof shading, and applicable metering. Instead of promising a fixed saving, we assess your bill to provide a realistic estimate.
How Long Does It Take to Recover the Investment? ▼
Payback period varies by project (typically 3.2 to 4 years for residential and commercial). It depends on system cost, DISCOM tariff, solar generation, and subsidy.
How Many Solar Panels Do I Need? ▼
Panel count depends on required capacity and wattage (e.g. 540W / 550W panels). A 3 kW home system uses 5-6 panels.
How Much Rooftop Space Do I Need? ▼
A 1 kW solar system generally requires approx 80-100 sq. ft. of shadow-free roof space. A site survey evaluates exact usable space.
Is Solar Suitable for My Roof? ▼
It is suitable if your roof has adequate space, structural strength, south-facing orientation, and minimal shadow. We verify all factors during site survey.
How Long Does Solar Installation Take? ▼
Physical rooftop installation takes 1 to 2 days for residential setups. DISCOM net meter testing & commissioning takes 2 to 3 weeks.
Will Solar Installation Damage My Roof? ▼
No. We use chemical anchoring, weather-proof sealing, and HDG structural mounts specifically engineered to preserve roof waterproofing.
Can Solar Be Installed on an Existing House? ▼
Yes, rooftop solar can easily be retrofitted on existing residential homes, commercial buildings, and factories.
Is Solar Subsidy Available? ▼
Eligible residential homeowners receive up to ₹78,000 direct bank transfer subsidy under PM Surya Ghar scheme.
Is Subsidy Available for Commercial / Industrial Projects? ▼
Residential schemes have specific domestic eligibility. Commercial & industrial projects benefit from 40% Accelerated Tax Depreciation and lower LCOE rather than direct retail subsidies.
What Is Net Metering? ▼
Net metering is a bi-directional DISCOM grid arrangement that imports power when needed and exports excess solar power generated during peak day hours, crediting your monthly bill.
What Happens to Solar Power During a Power Cut? ▼
On-grid systems shut down during outages to prevent anti-islanding grid danger. If backup power is required, a hybrid inverter with battery storage continues powering loads.
Will Solar Work at Night? ▼
Panels do not generate electricity at night. Night-time power is drawn from the grid (offset by daytime export credits) or from your battery storage.
Do I Need Batteries With Solar? ▼
Not necessarily. Standard on-grid systems operate without batteries, offering lower upfront cost and maximum bill savings.
What Maintenance Does a Solar System Need? ▼
Solar systems require periodic panel washing every 2-4 weeks to remove dust, inverter health checks, and annual electrical inspection.
What Warranty Is Available? ▼
Solar panels carry a 25-30 year performance warranty. Inverters carry 5-10 years warranty, and mounting structures carry 10 years warranty.
What Happens If Something Stops Working? ▼
Our local service team in Bhubaneswar & Chhattisgarh provides on-site diagnosis, array testing, and manufacturer warranty coordination.
Can Solar Be Installed on Metal or Sloping Roofs? ▼
Yes, specialized mounting structures are installed for RCC flat roofs, metal trapezoidal sheets, and sloping tiled structures.
Can Solar Be Installed on Open Land? ▼
Ground-mounted solar is ideal for factories or land owners with suitable open area, good solar exposure, and HT grid connectivity.
Do You Provide Solar Installation in Bhubaneswar & Odisha? ▼
Yes, we serve Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Puri, Sambalpur, Jharsuguda, and applicable service areas across Odisha.
Do You Provide Solar Installation in Chhattisgarh? ▼
Yes, we serve Raipur, Bhilai, Durg, Bilaspur, and applicable service areas across Chhattisgarh.
