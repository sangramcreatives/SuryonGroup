OUR ☀️ SOLAR SERVICES

Everything You Need to Go Solar

☀️ 01. Solar Consultation

☀️ Start With the Right Solar Advice

Not sure whether solar is suitable for your property or how much capacity you need? Our consultation helps you understand options before making an investment: consumption patterns, system options, savings, incentives & space.

📐 02. Site Survey & Assessment

Know Your Property Before Installing

A proper site assessment evaluates available rooftop area, structural load capacity, azimuth orientation, shading obstacles, and electrical infrastructure.

⚙️ 03. Solar System Design

⚡ Designed Around Your Energy Needs

Custom CAD layout engineering balancing energy generation, roof space, wind speed rating, inverter matching, and project economics.

🏗️ 04. Solar Installation

Professional ☀️ Solar Installation

Chemical anchoring, HDG structure mounting, Tier-1 DCR panel stringing, inverter wiring, LA earthing, and safety commissioning executed by certified technicians.

🏢 05. Solar EPC Services

End-to-End ☀️ Solar EPC Solutions

Turnkey Engineering, Procurement & Construction for commercial buildings, factories, warehouses, and MW-scale industrial rooftop projects.

⚡ 06. Net Metering Assistance

DISCOM Net Metering Approval

Complete guidance for TPCODL/TPSODL/CSPDCL portal documentation, meter testing coordination, and bi-directional solar meter installation.

🏛️ 07. Solar Subsidy Assistance

Understand Government Incentives

End-to-end assistance under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana for direct bank credit subsidy up to ₹78,000 for eligible residential homes.

🧹 08. Solar Maintenance

Keep Your System Performing

Periodic panel washing, dust cleaning, electrical connection torque checking, array health scans, and inverter performance checks.

🛠️ 09. Solar AMC

Annual 🧹 Maintenance Contracts

Comprehensive annual maintenance plans with scheduled quarterly site visits, priority breakdown response, panel cleaning & inverter servicing.

📊 10. Solar System Monitoring

Know How Your System Performs

Mobile app & SCADA remote monitoring integration to track daily kWh generation, fault alerts, and performance trends in real-time.

🛡️ 11. Solar Insurance

Protect Your Solar Investment

Comprehensive coverage policies protecting your system against natural disasters, theft, accidental damage, and generation loss. Secure your ROI with specialized solar insurance.

✨ 12. Solar Cleaning Services

Maximize Energy Generation

Professional RO water cleaning, automated scrubbing, and dust-removal services designed to boost panel efficiency. Dust and dirt can reduce output by up to 30%—let our team keep it shining.

🧰 13. Solar Cleaning Equipments

DIY & Automated Cleaning Tools

We supply high-quality telescopic brushes, water-fed poles, robotic solar cleaners, and specialized soft bristles to help you safely clean your panels on your own.
